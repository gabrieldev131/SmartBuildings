File: `vision/featureExtractor.py`
Purpose: Computes 576-dimensional L2-normalized multi-stripe appearance embeddings combining
MobileNetV3-Small deep semantics, mid-level clothing texture, and illumination-invariant
Gray-World white-balanced color/chromaticity descriptors.

- Lines 8-14: Imports `numpy`, `torch`, `torch.nn.functional as F`, `torchvision.models`, `roi_align`, and `Config`.
- Lines 16-18: Reads `Config.USE_GPU`, selects `cuda:0` if available (else `cpu`), and sets `_use_fp16 = True` when running on CUDA.
- Lines 20-26: Loads pretrained `MobileNet_V3_Small_Weights.DEFAULT` convolutional feature extractor, moves it to `_device` in eval mode (and `.half()` on GPU), and splits it into:
  * `_backbone_mid = _backbone[:9]`: Layers 0..8 producing `(N, 48, 8, 4)` mid-level texture maps from `(N, 3, 128, 64)` crops.
  * `_backbone_deep = _backbone[9:]`: Layers 9..12 producing `(N, 576, 4, 2)` deep semantic feature maps.
- Lines 28-30: Allocates ImageNet normalization tensors `_mean = [0.485, 0.456, 0.406]` and `_std = [0.229, 0.224, 0.225]` shaped `(1, 3, 1, 1)`.
- Lines 32-39: Pre-warms common batch sizes `(1, 4, 8, ..., 128)` on GPU with `_pad_buf` so cuDNN finishes kernel autotuning at startup rather than stalling during live video.

Function: `extract_multistripe_embeddings_from_maps(crops_rgb_01, mid_map, deep_map)` (Lines 42-132)
- Lines 55-56: Computes global semantic summary `g_avg` (68% spatial mean + 32% spatial max over the `4x2` deep map, shape `(N, 576)`) and 4 horizontal stripes `s4_raw` (68% width mean + 32% width max, shape `(N, 576, 4)`).
- Lines 58-59: Pads `s4_raw` with replicate mode (`s4_pad`) and blends each horizontal stripe (`65%` self + `17.5%` upper neighbor + `17.5%` lower neighbor). This vertical smoothing makes the embedding invariant to 10-15% vertical bounding-box framing or camera-pitch differences between cameras.
- Lines 61-66: Compresses the 576 channels per stripe into 144 channels (`s4.view(-1, 4, 144, 4).mean(dim=1)`), flattens the 4 stripes back into `576D` (`deep_stripes`), zero-centers both `deep_stripes` and `g_avg`, and fuses them (`F.normalize(deep_stripes) + 0.55 * F.normalize(g_centered)`) into `deep_part` (`(N, 576)`).
- Lines 69-74: Extracts the Mid-Level Texture branch (`mid_part`, `(N, 192)`):
  * Line 69 (`mid_inner`): Weights the inner 2 horizontal columns `[:, :, :, 1:3]` (the person's torso/legs) by `0.82` and the full width by `0.18` so background walls or adjacent people at the box edges are suppressed.
  * Lines 70-72: Pools `mid_inner` into 4 fine horizontal stripes (`mid_4`) and 2 coarse horizontal stripes (`mid_2`, repeated to match shape), blending `0.70 * mid_4 + 0.30 * mid_2`.
  * Lines 73-74: Zero-centers and L2-normalizes into `mid_part` (`192D`).
- Lines 77-120: Extracts the Illumination-Invariant Color & Chromaticity branch (`col_part`, `(N, 192)`):
  * Line 77 (`body = crops_rgb_01[:, :, 14:114, 16:48].float()`): Crops the inner torso/legs region (vertical rows `14..114` out of `128`, horizontal columns `16..48` out of `64`) to exclude background floor/walls and adjacent people.
  * Lines 81-84 (Gray-World White-Balance Normalization): Computes per-crop mean per RGB channel `ch_mean` (clamped to `[0.10, 0.92]`) and overall luminance mean `lum_mean`, then scales `wb_body = (body * (lum_mean / ch_mean)).clamp(0.0, 1.0)`. This removes warm/cool lighting tints across different cameras.
  * Lines 86-88: Computes raw luminance `lum = 0.299*R + 0.587*G + 0.114*B` and contrast-normalizes it (`lum_norm`) using per-crop mean and standard deviation so exposure changes across cameras do not shift brightness features.
  * Lines 90-102: Computes white-balanced opponent color channels `rg = (rw - gw) * 1.35`, `yb = (0.5*(rw + gw) - bw) * 1.35`, raw chromaticity ratios `(rn, gn, bn)`, white-balanced chromaticity ratios `(rwn, gwn, bwn)`, and HSV-like color saturation `sat = (cmax - cmin) / (cmax + 0.01)`.
  * Lines 105-107 (`col_stack`): Concatenates all 12 illumination-robust channels along `dim=1`.
  * Line 109 (`col_core`): Pools horizontally with 75% weight on the innermost 16 pixels (`8:24`) and 25% on the 32-pixel torso width.
  * Lines 110-117: Computes 8-stripe average pooling (`col_avg_8`, `96D`), 4-stripe coarse pooling interpolated to 8 stripes (`col_coarse_4`, `96D`), and 8-stripe max pooling (`col_max_8`, `96D`), concatenating them into a `192D` multi-scale color vector `col_feat`.
  * Lines 118-119: Zero-centers and L2-normalizes into `col_part` (`192D`).
- Lines 122-125: Fuses `deep_part`, `mid_part`, and `col_part` into three 192D blocks (`b1`, `b2`, `0.95 * col_part`) and applies final L2 normalization to return the `(N, 576)` unit embedding tensor.

Functions: `compute_part_based_embeddings`, `extract_features_batch_from_frame`, `extract_deep_reid_feature` (Lines 135-200)
- Lines 135-157 (`compute_part_based_embeddings`): Standalone wrapper that normalizes `(N, 3, 128, 64)` crops using `_mean`/`_std`, pads to pre-warmed bucket sizes on GPU if `n <= 128`, runs `_backbone_mid` and `_backbone_deep`, and calls `extract_multistripe_embeddings_from_maps`.
- Lines 160-176 (`extract_features_batch_from_frame`): Extracts `(K, 576)` embeddings for all boxes in a single BGR NumPy frame using `roi_align` + `compute_part_based_embeddings`.
- Lines 179-198 (`extract_deep_reid_feature`): Extracts a single `(576,)` embedding for one bounding box `[x1, y1, x2, y2]` using `F.interpolate` + `compute_part_based_embeddings`.
- Lines 201-203: Backward-compatible function aliases (`extract_color_histogram`, etc.).
